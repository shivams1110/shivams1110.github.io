How to play

  1. Tap New game. Swipe on the board to steer your snake toward apples.
  2. Eat apples to grow and earn points. Collect them quickly to build combos, and look out for power-ups.
  3. Avoid the edges, your own body, and fire. Tap the pause icon when you need a break.

Prefer buttons? Open Customize → Direction buttons. You can adjust Button size or use Set button positions to move the arrows, then tap Save positions.

Quick answers

How do I change my snake or background?

Open Customize, then choose Snake skin or Background. Select a style to preview it. If it’s locked, you’ll need enough coins to buy it. Confirm the purchase with your in-game coins, then tap Apply.

A rewarded video won’t load, or I didn’t receive coins.

Check your internet connection and try again later. An ad may not be available for every request. To receive the reward, watch the video through to completion and close it when prompted. If a completed video didn’t grant its reward, email us with the approximate time and what happened.

The game has no sound.

Open Customize and turn Sound on. Check your device’s volume and whether audio is playing through connected headphones or speakers.

The game freezes, crashes, or doesn’t respond.

Close and reopen the app, then check the App Store or Google Play for an available update. If the problem continues, email us with your device model, OS version, app version, and the steps that caused it.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Coins, unlocked styles, scores, and settings are saved locally on your device. The game doesn’t currently have player accounts or an in-app cloud-sync or progress-transfer feature.

How can I ask about privacy or my data?

Email devtechindia11@gmail.com with your question. You don’t need a player account to contact us.

Before reinstalling

Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data can remove your saved progress and unlocked items. Contact support before doing this if you want help troubleshooting a problem.
