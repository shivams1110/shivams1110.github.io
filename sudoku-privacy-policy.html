Your puzzles stay on your device.

You can play without an account or an internet connection. Saved games, notes and progress are stored locally. When ads are enabled, Google AdMob processes separate device and usage information as explained below.

Data stored on your device

Sudoku Nest saves the following information locally so you can continue playing and keep your preferences:

  • Saved puzzles, entries, pencil notes and game state.
  • Level progress and game statistics.
  • Preferences such as difficulty, mistake rules, sound, vibration and solving assistance.
  • Hint allowances and earned rewards, so the app can restore them with your saved game.

This data is used to restore saved games, show your progress and remember your settings.

Collection and sharing

Sudoku Nest does not require registration or ask you to provide your name or email address to play. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

The developer does not receive, sell or share your locally stored gameplay data. Sudoku Nest does not operate a developer-hosted server for saved games or account data.

Local game storage is separate from the information processed by the advertising services described below. The app does not include a separate developer-operated analytics service.

Information you voluntarily send by email is handled separately, as described under Contact and support.

Advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, Sudoku Nest uses Google AdMob for banner ads, occasional ads between puzzles, and rewarded ads you can choose to watch for an extra hint or an extra chance in Limited mode. Ordinary solving and three free hints per puzzle work offline. Ads require a connection and may not always be available.

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share the following information to deliver and measure advertising, understand service performance and prevent fraud:

  • Your IP address, which can indicate your general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Usage and interaction information, such as app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostics about app and advertising performance.

Processing depends on the advertising configuration and your privacy choices. It does not include sending your saved puzzle boards or pencil notes to the developer. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising.

Where required, the app asks for your privacy choices using Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit the form through App Settings > Ad privacy choices when that option is available. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings; labels vary by device.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. You can continue solving or restart a puzzle without watching one.

Device services and backups

Sound and vibration use your device’s built-in services. You can adjust these features in the app’s Settings.

Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device and account settings. These services are controlled by your platform provider and follow its privacy policies. The developer does not receive or control those backups.

Keep your device secure and review its backup settings if you want to control where copies of your game data are stored.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is replaced through normal gameplay, cleared or removed with the app.

On Android, open your device’s Settings, select Apps, choose Sudoku Nest, then open Storage or Storage & cache and select Clear storage or Clear data. The exact labels may vary by device. This removes saved games, progress, statistics and preferences from the local installation.

Uninstalling the app also removes its local installation data. Device or account backups may remain separately and may restore data when you reinstall. Manage those copies through your platform’s backup settings.

There is no Sudoku Nest account or developer-held gameplay record to delete. If you contact support, correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and any related follow-up. You can ask for its deletion using the contact address below; information may need to be retained where required by law.

Advertising data is handled under Google's retention and deletion practices. Clearing Sudoku Nest's local storage does not itself delete information already processed by Google. Visit Google's data retention information for details.

Security and service providers

Game saves use the app's local storage, protected by your device's operating system. Keep your device secure, especially if other people can access it.

Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Your email service and the developer's email provider handle support messages. Those providers, advertising services and device backup providers process information under their own policies and may process it in countries other than your own.

If you visit this policy online, the hosting provider may receive your IP address and standard request information to serve the page. This page itself does not include advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies.

Children’s privacy

Sudoku Nest does not ask players to create an account or submit personal details to play. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through support correspondence. Advertising services, when enabled, can process device and usage information as described above; local puzzle storage does not mean all use of the app is free of data processing.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information with the developer, contact the address below so the matter can be reviewed and the information removed as appropriate.

Contact and support
