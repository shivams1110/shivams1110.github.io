Your puzzles stay on your device.

Sudoku Nest works without an account. The app stores game data locally and does not send your gameplay information to the developer. It has no advertising or analytics integration.

Data stored on your device

Sudoku Nest saves the following information locally so you can continue playing and keep your preferences:

  • Saved puzzles, entries, pencil notes and game state.
  • Level progress and game statistics.
  • Preferences such as difficulty, mistake rules, sound, vibration and solving assistance.

This data is used to restore saved games, show your progress and remember your settings.

Collection and sharing

The app does not require registration or ask for your name, email address, contacts or location. It does not include advertising, analytics or remote data collection services.

The developer does not receive, sell or share your locally stored gameplay data. Sudoku Nest does not operate a developer-hosted server for saved games or account data.

Information you voluntarily send by email is handled separately, as described under Contact and support.

Device services and backups

Sound and vibration use your device’s built-in services. You can adjust these features in the app’s Settings.

Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to your device and account settings. These services are controlled by your platform provider and follow its privacy policies. The developer does not receive or control those backups.

Keep your device secure and review its backup settings if you want to control where copies of your game data are stored.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is replaced through normal gameplay, cleared or removed with the app.

On Android, open your device’s Settings, select Apps, choose Sudoku Nest, then open Storage or Storage & cache and select Clear storage or Clear data. The exact labels may vary by device. This removes saved games, progress, statistics and preferences from the local installation.

Uninstalling the app also removes its local installation data. Device or account backups may remain separately and may restore data when you reinstall. Manage those copies through your platform’s backup settings.

There is no Sudoku Nest account or developer-held gameplay record to delete. To ask about information sent in a support email, contact the developer at the address below.

Children’s privacy

The app does not ask players for their age or collect personal information through gameplay. The same local storage practices apply to all players.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer in a support message, please email the developer to request its removal.

Contact and support

If you email the developer, your email address and any information you choose to include are handled through the developer’s email service to respond to your request. Only send information needed to explain your question.
