Privacy Policy

Classic Snake Deluxe (Android / iOS)
Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy describes how Classic Snake Deluxe (“the app”), published by UMDA (package com.umda.classic_snake_deluxe), handles information. The app is a single-player snake game. We do not ask you to create an account.

What we store on your device

Game progress stays on your device using local storage (coins, unlocked snake skins and backgrounds, selected look, and whether sound is muted). This data is not uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What third parties may collect

The app uses Google services. Those companies process data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — banner ads during play and optional rewarded video ads that grant in-game coins. Ads may use your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location derived from IP, to show and measure ads (including personalized ads where allowed by your device settings and law).
  • Google Firebase Analytics — automatic app usage and device information (for example app opens, device model, OS version, country) to understand stability and engagement. We do not log your name, email, or a player account.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports (stack traces, device model, OS version) so we can fix bugs.
  • Google Fonts — the app may download fonts from Google, which can see your IP address as part of that request.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from ads.

Permissions

The app needs Internet to load ads, analytics, crash reports, and fonts. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the store listing email and we will delete it where we can.

Your choices

  • You can skip rewarded ads; they are optional. Banner ads may still appear during a round on supported devices.
  • You can limit ad tracking in Android or iOS system settings (advertising ID / App Tracking Transparency where shown).
  • You can delete local game data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your information. We do not share player data with other companies except as needed for the Google services above, to comply with law, or to protect the app and users.

Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google’s retention rules for AdMob, Analytics, and Crashlytics.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email on the Classic Snake Deluxe listing on Google Play or the App Store.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
